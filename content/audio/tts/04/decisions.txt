D: Part three. You will hear five people talking about a decision they almost didn't make. For each speaker, choose what they say about it.
D: Speaker one.
B: Three years ago I was applying for jobs in finance, and I sent my CV to about forty companies. With one of them, I typed the email address wrong, and it went to a games studio with an almost identical name. I'd never even heard of them. Two days later they rang me and said, if you can do spreadsheets like this, come and work for us. If I hadn't made that typo, I'd be sitting in a bank right now, probably bored to death.
D: Speaker two.
G: I couldn't swim until I was forty-one. I'd always told myself I'd learn when I had more time. Honestly, if only I'd started at twenty! Think of all the summers I spent sitting on the beach, watching everyone else.
D: Speaker three.

D: Speaker four.

If she hadn't been there, I'd have turned back on the second day.
D: Speaker five.
A: I was flying to my best friend's wedding in Dublin, and I'd left my passport on the kitchen table. I only realised at the airport. I'd have missed the flight if he hadn't stopped, and I wouldn't have been her bridesmaid. I still send him a card every Christmas.
